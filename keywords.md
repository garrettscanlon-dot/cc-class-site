## How to Read This Report

- **Volume:** Monthly US search volume (Google)
- **Competition:** Google Ads competition index (0–1). Lower = fewer advertisers bidding = easier to rank
- **CPC:** Cost-per-click if running Google Ads. High CPC = high commercial intent but expensive to buy
- **Opportunity Score:** Custom score: `volume × (1 - competition)`. Higher = better bang for buck
- Keywords are grouped by strategic theme aligned to the MMP value proposition

## Recommended Actions

### 1. Create dedicated landing pages for Tier 1 keywords
- **"Customer health score"** (260 vol, 0.44 comp) — Write a definitive guide: "What Is a Customer Health Score? How to Build One That Actually Predicts Churn." Link to MMP as the tool that automates this.
- **"Customer churn prevention"** (140 vol, 0.02 comp) — This is nearly uncontested. Create a landing page: "Customer Churn Prevention: The Monday Morning System."
- **"Renewal management software"** (110 vol, 0.18 comp) — Position MMP's renewal tracking as a lighter alternative to full CS platforms.

### 2. Build comparison pages to intercept competitor traffic
- "Monday Morning Playbook vs Gainsight" — target the 210 vol for "gainsight customer success platform"
- "Monday Morning Playbook vs Totango" and "vs ChurnZero"
- These are high-intent buyers actively evaluating alternatives

### 3. Publish weekly content targeting long-tail keywords
- "Customer health score template" (40 vol) — offer a free template, gate with email
- "Customer health score metrics" (20 vol) — define the metrics MMP tracks
- "Customer success onboarding process" (50 vol) — how MMP simplifies week-one onboarding
- "Customer churn prediction and prevention" (50 vol) — thought leadership piece

### 4. Own the "weekly" and "Monday" keywords
- These barely exist in search yet — MMP can define the category
- Create content around "customer success weekly report" and "Monday morning CS ritual"
- As MMP grows, these searches will grow with it

### 5. Avoid overspending on head terms
- "Customer success software" ($116 CPC) and "customer success platform" ($72 CPC) are expensive to buy
- Invest in SEO content instead — comparison guides, "best of" lists, case studies
- Use these as long-term organic targets, not paid campaigns

## Key Takeaways

1. **"Customer health score" is the single best keyword cluster** — 260 monthly searches, medium competition, and it's exactly what MMP does. Write the definitive guide.
2. **"Customer churn prevention" is wide open** — 140 searches/mo with near-zero competition. This should be a landing page within a week.
3. **The "weekly workflow" category barely exists in search** — this is both a risk (low volume) and an opportunity (MMP can define the category). Build content now so you own it as the market catches up.
4. **Competitor comparison pages are a must** — "Gainsight alternatives" and similar queries have strong volume. MMP's speed and simplicity story is perfect for these.
5. **Don't buy Google Ads for head terms** — CPCs above $70 make paid acquisition uneconomical at MMP's price point. Invest in organic content instead.
