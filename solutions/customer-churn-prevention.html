The Problem

Churn doesn't happen overnight.
It happens while you're not looking.

Most CS teams don't have a churn problem — they have a visibility problem. By the time risk surfaces, it's too late to act.

Churn signals buried in 5+ tools

Usage data in one dashboard, support tickets in another, NPS in a spreadsheet. No single view of account health means at-risk accounts stay hidden until it's too late.

Risk caught too late to save the account

Your CSM finds out an account is churning when the cancellation email arrives — not when usage dropped 40% three weeks ago. Reactive firefighting replaces proactive retention.

No consistent methodology across CSMs

Every CSM assesses risk differently. One uses gut feel, another checks Salesforce, a third looks at support tickets. Without a shared framework, churn prevention is a coin flip.
