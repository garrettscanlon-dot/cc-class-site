The Problem

Why you need a customer health score

Without a structured scoring framework, your team is guessing which accounts need attention. Here is what that looks like in practice.

!
Vibes-based risk assessment fails at scale
When your team manages 20 accounts each, gut instinct works. At 40 or more, critical churn signals slip through the cracks because no one can hold that much context in their head.
?
Inconsistent scoring across CSMs creates blind spots
One CSM flags an account as "at risk" based on usage. Another looks at NPS. A third checks support tickets. Without a shared framework, your team's assessments are incomparable.

You need a framework before you need a tool
Buying a CS platform before defining what "healthy" means for your accounts is like automating a broken process. Get the framework right first, then automate it.

Implementation

How to use this template

Four steps to go from no health scoring to a consistent, team-wide framework.

1

Define Dimensions

Start with the five dimensions above. Remove any that don't apply to your product, and add domain-specific ones if needed.

2

Assign Weights

Weight each dimension based on how predictive it is of churn or expansion. Product usage is typically the strongest signal.

3

Set Thresholds

Define green, yellow, and red thresholds with your team. Use historical data to calibrate. Adjust as you learn.

4

Review Weekly

Review scores every Monday. Use them to prioritize outreach, flag at-risk accounts, and surface expansion opportunities.
