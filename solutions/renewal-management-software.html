The Problem

Your renewal process has blind spots

Most CS teams find out about renewal risk too late -- after the customer has already decided to leave.

Renewals buried in spreadsheets

Your renewal timeline lives in a Google Sheet that only one person updates. Dates slip, ownership is unclear, and nothing is connected to actual account health data.

No early warning system

By the time someone flags a renewal as "at risk," the customer has already gone dark. There are no automated health signals feeding into your renewal workflow.

Expansion opportunities found too late

Usage spikes, seat growth, and champion activity happen months before the renewal -- but nobody sees them until the window has already closed.
