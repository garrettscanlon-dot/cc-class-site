The Problem

Why Traditional Churn Prediction Fails CS Teams

You bought the tool. Built the model. Still lost the account.

⏲

Prediction models need months of training data

Most ML-based churn tools require 6-12 months of historical data before they produce usable predictions. You need answers now, not next year.

⚠

False positives waste CSM time

Model-based scoring floods your team with noise. When every account looks "at risk," your CSMs stop trusting the tool and go back to gut instinct.

⏱

By the time the model alerts you, the customer is already gone

Probability scores update slowly and lack context. By the time a churn score crosses the threshold, the customer has already made their decision.

How MMP Works

Signal-Driven Risk Scoring, Not Black-Box Models

MMP doesn't try to predict the future with a probability score. It reads the signals your customers are already sending and turns them into a prioritized weekly action list.

  • ✓
    Usage drops detected Product usage trending down over 2+ weeks triggers a risk flag automatically.
  • ✓
    NPS & sentiment shifts Declining NPS scores and negative Gong call sentiment surface before renewal conversations.
  • ✓
    Missed contact cadence Accounts that go silent get flagged. No response in 3+ weeks means it's time to act.
  • ✓
    Renewal timeline context Risk signals are weighted by renewal proximity. An at-risk account renewing in 30 days gets top priority.
